# Visual Reference Checklist

The supplied screenshots are the reference direction for the application's visual language.

## Preserve the feel

- Warm white page background.
- White content surfaces.
- Deep green as the primary accent.
- Dark charcoal headings.
- Muted gray-green secondary text.
- Compact left navigation.
- Top utility/search area.
- Rounded cards, but not excessive.
- Strong alignment and whitespace.
- Small status labels.
- Simple, readable charts.
- One or two visually prominent areas rather than everything competing.

## Adapt it to Antarctic operations

Instead of:
- projects
- tasks
- meetings
- team members

Use:
- station health
- active alerts
- energy balance
- inventory
- environmental conditions
- asset health
- forecast
- operator actions

## Avoid looking copied

Change:
- navigation labels
- card compositions
- chart types
- information hierarchy
- iconography
- station map/twin visualization
- domain terminology

The result should feel like an original remote-operations product that happens to share the reference's clean editorial aesthetic.
